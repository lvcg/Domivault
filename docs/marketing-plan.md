# DomiVault Marketing Plan

## Purpose

DomiVault helps homeowners keep receipts, warranties, maintenance records, repair history, vendors, expenses, bills, and vehicle records organized before they need them.

This plan starts from zero users and focuses on simple, repeatable growth channels that can be run by a small team without a large ad budget.

Live site: https://www.domivaultapp.com  
App category: Home management, productivity, personal records, property maintenance  
Primary audience: homeowners, renters, landlords, property managers, first-time home buyers, DIY homeowners, and families managing household records

## Positioning

### Core Message

DomiVault is a home command center and records vault for everything your home needs: receipts, warranties, maintenance reminders, expenses, vendors, repairs, and reports.

### Simple Value Proposition

Most homeowners only look for receipts, warranty papers, contractor details, or repair history when something breaks. DomiVault keeps those records organized in one secure place before the stressful moment happens.

### Tagline Options

- Your home command center and records vault.
- Keep every home record ready before you need it.
- Track repairs, receipts, warranties, and maintenance in one place.
- Home records, maintenance, and proof organized.

## 0-User Launch Strategy

### Phase 1: Foundation

Goal: make the product easy to understand, trust, and test.

Actions:

- Confirm the home page clearly explains what DomiVault does in the first screen.
- Add screenshots or short GIFs showing dashboard, scanner, expenses, appliances, and reports.
- Keep Privacy Policy and Terms easy to find.
- Add a clear tester invitation page or post with instructions.
- Create a short demo video showing:
  - create an account
  - add an expense
  - add an appliance
  - scan a receipt
  - create a maintenance reminder
  - export a report

Success metrics:

- 25 tester signups
- 10 users complete onboarding
- 5 users create at least one real record
- 3 pieces of useful feedback

### Phase 2: Community Testing

Goal: get real user feedback before spending on ads.

Actions:

- Recruit testers from personal network, homeowner groups, Reddit, Facebook, and local community groups.
- Offer mutual app testing for other founders.
- Ask testers for specific feedback instead of general opinions.
- Track bugs and feature requests in Jira.

Tester ask:

> I'm testing DomiVault, a home command center for receipts, warranties, maintenance reminders, expenses, vendors, and repair records. I'm looking for homeowners or renters willing to try it and share feedback. I'm happy to test your app, site, or project in return.

Success metrics:

- 50 total testers
- 20 weekly active users
- 10 users create 3 or more records
- top 5 bugs identified and fixed

### Phase 3: Organic Content

Goal: attract homeowners already searching for organization, repair, warranty, and maintenance help.

Content themes:

- "What records should homeowners keep?"
- "Home maintenance checklist by month"
- "How long should you keep appliance receipts?"
- "How to organize warranties and manuals"
- "What to track after a home repair"
- "What to do before a warranty expires"
- "Home expense tracker template"
- "First-time homeowner maintenance checklist"

Formats:

- Blog posts
- Reddit discussion posts
- Facebook group posts
- Pinterest graphics
- YouTube Shorts or TikTok demos
- Google Business-style help articles

Success metrics:

- 4 helpful posts per month
- 1 short video per week
- 100 organic site visits per month
- 5 signups from organic content

## Reddit Strategy

Reddit should be treated as a listening and credibility channel first, not a direct advertising channel. Many communities remove direct promotion, and rules vary by subreddit. Check each subreddit rules page before posting.

### Reddit Principles

- Be useful before linking.
- Do not post the same promotional message across many communities.
- Ask for feedback, not sales.
- Share lessons learned, checklists, or templates.
- Use launch/showcase threads when available.
- Avoid posting from an account with only promotional history.

### Reddit Communities To Research

Check rules before posting:

- `r/homeowners`
- `r/FirstTimeHomeBuyer`
- `r/HomeImprovement`
- `r/DIY`
- `r/realestateinvesting`
- `r/Landlord`
- `r/propertymanagement`
- `r/personalfinance`
- `r/frugal`
- `r/productivity`
- `r/SideProject`
- `r/SaaS`
- `r/startups`
- `r/Entrepreneur`
- `r/androidapps`
- `r/AppFeedback`

### Reddit Post Angles

Feedback post:

> I built a home records app because I kept seeing homeowners lose receipts, warranty details, and repair history when something broke. I'm looking for feedback from homeowners, renters, or landlords. What would you want a home maintenance and document vault to track?

Useful checklist post:

> I made a checklist of records homeowners should keep after repairs: receipt, vendor, date, warranty expiration, model number, serial number, before/after photos, and next service date. What else would you add?

Mutual testing post:

Launch post:

> I launched DomiVault, a home command center for receipts, warranties, expenses, maintenance reminders, vendors, and repair records. I'm looking for early testers and blunt feedback before wider release.

## Google Marketing Strategy

Google should focus on intent-based discovery: people searching because they have a problem right now.

### SEO Targets

High-intent keywords:

- home maintenance tracker
- home expense tracker
- appliance warranty tracker
- receipt organizer for homeowners
- home repair record app
- home maintenance checklist app
- home document vault
- property maintenance tracker
- landlord maintenance records
- home inventory and warranty tracker

### Google Ads Starter Test

Start with Search ads before broad display ads.

Campaign ideas:

- Home Maintenance Tracker
- Receipt And Warranty Organizer
- Home Expense Tracker
- Landlord Repair Records

Landing page focus:

- One primary CTA: create account or join tester group.
- Show screenshots immediately.
- Explain Free vs Plus clearly.
- Include Privacy Policy and Terms.

App campaign note:

After the Google Play listing is live, Google App campaigns can promote installs across Search, Google Play, YouTube, Discover, and the Display Network. Start small and optimize toward installs first, then paid upgrades once conversion data exists.

## Messaging Library

### Short Description

DomiVault keeps home records, repairs, warranties, and expenses organized.

### Full Pitch

DomiVault is a secure home command center and records vault for homeowners, renters, landlords, and property managers. Track expenses, bills, projects, appliances, warranties, maintenance tasks, vendors, vehicles, receipts, and reports in one organized place.

### Problem Statement

Home records are usually scattered across drawers, email, text messages, folders, and memory. When something breaks, homeowners often cannot quickly find proof of purchase, warranty details, service history, or the right vendor.

### Solution Statement

DomiVault gives each home a central place to organize maintenance, documents, expenses, vendors, repairs, and reminders so important records are ready when needed.
